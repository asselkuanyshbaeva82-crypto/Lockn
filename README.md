# Lockn — Student Productivity & Focus Platform

Lockn is a student productivity platform designed to help students **stay focused, organized, and motivated**. It combines study planning, focus sessions, task management, progress tracking, and social challenges in one application.

##  Features

*  **Focus Mode** — Start focused study sessions and reduce distractions.
*  **To-Do List** — Create, organize, and complete daily study tasks.
*  **Calendar** — Plan study sessions, assignments, exams, and important deadlines.
*  **Progress Tracking** — Track study time, completed tasks, goals, and productivity.
*  **Streaks** — Build consistent study habits by maintaining daily streaks.
*  **Teams** — Join or create study teams with other students.
*  **Competitions & Challenges** — Participate in study challenges and compete with friends.
*  **Statistics** — View your study activity and progress over time.
*  **Reminders** — Get notifications about upcoming tasks and study sessions.
*  **User Profiles** — Manage personal goals, achievements, statistics, and team activity.

## Goal

The main goal of YTP is to make studying **more structured, engaging, and motivating**.

Instead of using separate applications for planning, timers, task management, and study groups, students can manage everything in one platform.
